The approach

A funnel converts. A flywheel compounds.

You still need a strong funnel to turn interest into registrations and registrations into attendance. The bigger opportunity is making every event feed the audience, partners and content of the next one, city by city.

  1. Know the audienceSegment in HubSpot by city, role, seniority and attendance history.
  2. Activate partners and communitySpeakers, communities and sponsors with agreed distribution and tracked links.
  3. Convert with a specific promiseA Luma page and message per segment: what an engineer gets is not what a CTO gets.
  4. Make people show upApproval-based registration, reminders, the agenda and who'll be in the room.
  5. Follow up differentlyOne track for attendees, another for no-shows, each with a clear next step.
  6. Turn attendees into a channelReturning guests, referrals, future speakers and partners.

First 180 days

DAYS 1–30

Understand

Audience, calendar, past results and current processes. Set baselines and pick a pilot event.

DAYS 31–60

Test

Run a segmented campaign with partners on a live event and document the communication flow.

DAYS 61–90

Playbook

Turn what worked into a repeatable playbook, from audience to page to campaigns to attendance, with a tracking dashboard.

DAYS 91–180

Prove

Apply it across live events: a rising attendance rate and a reusable audience in each city.

I won't promise percentages before seeing the baselines. The goal is a system that gets better with every event.

What I'd measure

  1. Right people?Share of registrants and attendees within the ICP
  2. Do they show up?Show-up rate by segment, channel and format
  3. Is it efficient?Cost per qualified attendee
  4. Do they come back?Repeat attendance rate
  5. Is it compounding?Share of registrations from the existing audience, referrals and partners

Built from public information and conversations so far. These are working hypotheses, and I'd validate them with the team first.
