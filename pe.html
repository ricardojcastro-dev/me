02Every event briefThe exact workflow, from brief to retro+

Repeating it in every city

ReuseThe same toolkitBrief template, Luma page template, email sequences, partner map format and scorecard, so no city starts from scratch.
LocaliseWhat changes per cityLocal partners, speakers and leaders, holidays and timing, language and the city's own audience list.
BenchmarkStart from the last cityAttendance rate, cost per attendee and the best channels from SF become the targets for London and New York.
CompoundKeep each city warmCity Luma calendars, local meetups and community content between events, so the next edition starts with an audience.

The workflow · T = event day

  1. T-14 wks1. BriefCity, date, capacity, attendance and decision-maker targets, budget.
  2. T-14 wks2. AudiencePast attendees and city lists from HubSpot; partner, speaker and leader lists.
  3. T-12 wks3. Event pageLuma page with design, content, qualifying questions and the team offer.
  4. T-12 → T-1 wk4. CampaignsDatabase invites, partners, paid and sponsored media, speaker and agenda drops.
  5. T-7 days → T5. AttendanceReconfirm or release, reminders, logistics, waitlist.
  6. T+1 → T+146. Follow-upAttendees and no-shows on separate tracks; vendor accounts to sales.
  7. T+147. RetroScorecard per channel and partner; update the playbook.
