Layer 1Every event briefThe exact workflow I'd follow each time a new brief lands+

From audience to event page to campaigns to attendance. One core workflow, with versions for PlatformCon (reactivating a known audience) and AgenticCon (a launch with new positioning).

  1. Brief intakeCapacity, date, target attendance rate, ICP mix and budget, agreed upfront.
  2. Audience pullCity lists in HubSpot, the city's Luma calendar subscribers, partners and community leaders.
  3. Event pageA Luma page with design and content, plus registration questions that qualify each attendee.
  4. CampaignsBriefs for performance and lifecycle, social, partners, speakers and community channels.
  5. Attendance engineApproval rules, reconfirm-or-release, reminders and waitlist backfill.
  6. Post-eventSeparate follow-ups for attendees and no-shows. Data goes back to HubSpot and the audience profile goes to sponsorship.
  7. RetroScore channels, partners and tests, then update the playbook.

Stage 1

Build the city audience

Start from the data we already have, then add partners and new channels.

  • HubSpot research and segmentation. Events attended, attended vs no-show, city, role, company.
  • ICP score and tiers. Fit, engagement and intent give tiers A, B and C.
  • Sync with performance and lifecycle. Goals set in qualified attendees. Lookalikes from tier A, LinkedIn targeting by title, email per tier.
  • City partners and leaders. Meetups, communities, newsletters and local leaders, with tracked codes.

Qualify & convert

A Luma page that converts, and registrations that tell us who's coming.

  • Event page with design and content. Strong visuals and copy per event.

Layer 5Web3 testingAn untapped audience I already know, and one small test to validate it+

Exchanges, custodians, payment companies and node, RPC and L2 infrastructure providers run large platform teams with the same problems: scale, reliability, security and developer experience. The focus is their infrastructure and platform teams, not smart contract developers.

Hypothesis

Same problems, untapped audience

A share of Web3 infra and platform leaders fit the PlatformCon and AgenticCon ICP but aren't in the community yet.

Test

One roundtable, one side event

"Platform engineering for blockchain infrastructure", next to a major Web3 conference (ETHGlobal, DevCon), plus a Web3 infra segment on an existing PlatformCon page.

Partners

Ecosystem distribution

Infra companies and ecosystem partners I've worked with as co-hosts, plus their engineering teams as speakers.

Decision

Scale only if it qualifies

Continue if 40%+ of registrants are tier A or B, the show-up rate is at or above average, and sponsors show interest. Otherwise stop.

Signal: I couldn't find any Web3-focused content or events in the Platform Engineering ecosystem. Yet Web3 companies like Consensys already hire platform engineers through your jobs board, and infrastructure providers like Blockdaemon talk about platform engineering. It looks like an untapped segment, worth one small test.
